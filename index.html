Before you start

Good questions.
Straight answers.

You shouldn’t need a sales call to understand the basics.

Ask a different question
Is this for a new bookkeeper or an established practice?

Both. You can start by researching potential clients, or bring an existing prospect or client and work on pricing, a proposal, or an engagement. You do not have to start with prospecting.

Can I try it without a credit card?

Yes. The Free plan lets you begin with up to 3 clients, 3 proposals and 3 engagement letters. Paid plans add capacity and capabilities. Review the current plan details before subscribing.

Will Evisor find clients who are ready to buy?

Evisor supports research into potential clients. A listed business or an apparent fit is not a confirmed request for bookkeeping. Coverage and available information vary; you still decide whom to approach and qualify their needs.

Do I need to move all my clients or connect my accounting software?

You can begin with one client or prospect rather than a full migration. Evisor handles the client-facing workflow around your work; it is not a replacement for your accounting ledger.

Will my clients need an Evisor account?

The public proposal and signing workflows use browser links, so recipients can review the document without a practitioner account. Any required consent and signature steps still apply.

Will it send outreach or documents without me reviewing them?

Prospect outreach and document delivery are different workflows. You choose the recipient and action. Review your draft and the sending options before proceeding, and configure any supported document reminders deliberately.

Can I work with clients outside my area?

Your service area and the availability of prospect data are separate questions. Check the coverage and supported jurisdictions in the application. Do not assume nationwide data availability or worldwide legal coverage.

What happens if I upgrade, downgrade or cancel?

Review the current billing terms before confirming a change. A plan change may affect charges, allowances and team access. For cancellation, refunds and document access, consult the Terms and Refund Policy.
