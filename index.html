Questions we'd ask if we were you.

Is the e-signature legally binding?

Digital signatures are legally binding in most countries. Evisor captures a full audit trail — name, timestamp, and IP — for every signed proposal.

How do payments work?

After your client signs, they see a payment button linked directly to your payment page — Stripe, PayPal, Wise, or whatever you already use. They click and pay.

Can I use my own engagement letter?

Absolutely. While we provide templates, you can copy/paste your existing terms into our editor and save them as your default.

Does this replace my practice management tool?

Evisor handles the proposal, signature, and billing. Once a client is signed up, you manage their actual work in whatever tool you already use.

How long does setup take?

Most users are sending their first proposal within 10 minutes. It's built for speed.

Is there a limit on proposals?

No. Every plan includes unlimited proposals. We want you to grow your business without worrying about per-document fees.
