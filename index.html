Note 2“What do I even charge?”

The costliest mistake in your practice isn’t in anyone’s books.

It’s in your quote. A bookkeeper on AccountingWEB once quoted five hours a quarter. After a software migration, the work took sixteen. Others are quieter: $50 or $100 a month, repeated every month the client stays.

$12,000 a yearThat’s 10 clients, each $100 a month under, for a year. Money you earned and never billed.

  • Describe the job.The state of the books, transactions, payroll, catch-up.

The things people actually ask.

Still unsure about something? Ask a person. A real one reads every message.

What do I get on the free plan?

Everything you need to set up and paper a real client: a names-masked preview of businesses near you (in covered states), the pricing calculator with your floor check, 3 clients, 3 proposals, and 3 engagement letters with e-signature and co-signers. No card, no time limit. Revealing a business’s name and contact details starts on Starter.

Will these businesses actually become clients?

We won’t quote a close rate. We can’t measure one honestly yet, and an invented number is worse than none. What we stand behind: the businesses are real listings in your area, each one shows why it’s there and what isn’t known, and when one says “send me something”, you can have a priced, branded proposal in front of them the same day. Finding clients still takes conversations. Evisor makes sure you start the right ones, and don’t lose one to a slow quote.

Isn’t this just another lead list?

A list hands you names. Evisor shows why each business is in front of you, which contact routes are on record, what isn’t known, and which details are old or conflicting. It won’t recycle duplicates or quietly widen your search to fill a quiet area. Find Clients is built on open business-listing data from the Overture Maps Foundation; what you’re paying for is the matching, the case for each business, the unlock that comes back if a promised route isn’t delivered, and everything after the yes. Other Evisor users near you may see the same businesses, but nobody is bidding on a quote request. You start the conversation, on your terms.

Which areas does Find Clients cover?

Today: Colorado, New York and Oregon. We’re opening state by state. Everything else in Evisor (pricing, proposals, engagement letters, e-signature, onboarding and renewals) isn’t limited to these states. If your state isn’t live yet, start free and use Notify me when this place is live in Find Clients to join the coverage update list.

Does Evisor contact businesses, or write my outreach?

No. You decide who to contact, what to say and how: from your own email, by phone or in person, so the relationship is yours from the first sentence. You’re the sender, so following the rules where you work is up to you (in the US, for example, CAN-SPAM for email). If you’d like help, Practice Build includes your outreach, written in your voice.

I’m brand new. Is this too much for me?

It’s built for exactly that moment. The pricing calculator works out a fee from your own rates, and you can send a real engagement letter for signature on the free plan. If you get stuck in your first week, we’ll build your first proposal with you on a call.

I don’t know what my rates should be yet. Can I still use it?

Yes. When you set your rates, you choose an experience level (such as a new practice), and Evisor shows a published wage benchmark for your market as context. It’s an employed rate, not a client fee, so treat it as a floor for your thinking. Start with a target and floor you’re comfortable with and change them any time. Every estimate shows the hourly rate a fee really pays you.

I already have clients and use QuickBooks or Xero. Does it fit?

Yes. Evisor sits alongside your bookkeeping software; the books stay where they are. Import your client list from a spreadsheet (name, email, business), paper everyone properly with engagement letters, and on Practice each letter comes up for renewal so you can review your whole book in one pass.

Are signatures captured in Evisor legally valid?

Evisor captures a simple electronic signature, recognized under laws including the US ESIGN Act, the UK Electronic Communications Act 2000 and the EU eIDAS Regulation. Each one is recorded with the disclosure shown, a fingerprint of the document, the IP address and the time. Some documents legally need a notarized or witnessed signature; don’t rely on any e-signature tool alone for those.

Do my clients need an account or an app?

No. They open a link on any device, read, then accept or sign. On a phone they can draw, upload or photograph a signature.

Can I cancel? Do you offer refunds?

Cancel any time; your plan stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Fees generally aren’t refunded once a billing period starts, which is why the free plan has no time limit: try the parts that matter to you before you pay. Full details are in the refund policy.

How is Practice different from Starter?

Starter is for winning your first clients: 75 reveals a month, your branding, payment links and up to 10 clients. Practice is for running the book: 250 reveals a month, 40 clients, the letter that fills itself from an accepted proposal, scheduled follow-ups, renewals and quick charges. Prices are in US dollars everywhere; sales tax or VAT, where it applies, is shown at checkout before you pay.
