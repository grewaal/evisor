The details matter.
Especially before you sign up.

Clear answers. No pretend guarantees.

What can I do on Free?

Start the document workflow with up to 3 clients, 3 proposals and 3 engagement letters. Paid plans add capacity and tools; prospect contact access and premium features are not all included in Free. Check current plan details before subscribing.

Does Evisor guarantee new clients?

No. Evisor helps you research and approach potential clients and prepare the next steps. A business listing is not a request for bookkeeping. Replies, suitability and client wins depend on real conversations and the work you offer.

Can I use my own wording and templates?

Yes, where included in your plan. Adapt the document to your practice, services and client. Review the scope and terms before sending; templates are starting points, not a substitute for advice about your engagement.

Will anything be sent or charged automatically?

Choosing a path on this page does not send a message, create an account or charge a card. In the application, review the sending or payment action and any configured reminder settings. A proposal, signed engagement and payment are different events.

What should I check about sensitive documents?

Before using Evisor for real client information, review its Privacy Policy, Terms and electronic-signature information, including access and retention arrangements. Make sure the document and signing process suit your engagement and jurisdiction.

What happens if I change plans or cancel?

Plan changes can affect charges, allowances and team access. The current plan selector and Terms explain the applicable conditions. Do not assume that cancellation erases records immediately or that storage and access continue indefinitely.

Read the Privacy Policy, Terms and Refund Policy for the applicable details.
