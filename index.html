How it works

From “who should I talk to?” to a signed engagement letter.

Six steps, one thread. Once a business becomes a client, its proposal, letter, onboarding and renewal stay on one record.

  1. 01Find

    Review businesses near you

    Pick your area and the kinds of clients you want. Read why each business is there, reveal the ones worth a conversation, then reach out your way — email, phone or a visit.

    Browse free · 75 reveals a month on Starter

  2. 02Price

    Know your number before they ask

    Set your target and floor rates once. Describe the job — state of the books, transactions, payroll, catch-up — and get a suggested monthly fee plus three options to present. If a price would pay you below your floor, Evisor says so first.

    Breakdown and floor check free on every plan

  3. 03Propose

    Send a proposal that looks like a firm sent it

    Turn the estimate into a proposal in your own branding. Your client reads the scope and fees on any device and accepts with their name. No login, no app.

    “Use in proposal” from Starter

  4. 04Sign

    Get the letter signed on their phone

    On Practice, the engagement letter fills itself from the accepted proposal. Your client draws, uploads or photographs their signature. Still unsigned after two days? Evisor sends the reminder.

    E-signature on every plan, co-signers included

  5. 05Onboard

    Start every client the same organized way

    Send one checklist link: software access, statements, prior returns, fee confirmation, a kickoff call. Your client ticks each item off and you see their progress.

    A checklist link you can copy and send

  6. 06Renew

    Review your whole book in one pass

    Twelve months after signing, each letter comes up for review. Choose a fee change, preview it, and prepare every renewal as a draft you check before it goes.

  • A record of what was signed

    Each signature is stored with the exact disclosure shown, a SHA-256 fingerprint of what was signed, the signer’s IP address and the UTC time. Evisor checks the signer saw the same version before it accepts the signature.

  • Signed means final

    Signed letters and accepted proposals can’t be edited afterwards — the database refuses. Changes happen in a new draft.

  • Encrypted where it matters

    Signatures and signed or accepted PDFs are encrypted with AES-GCM. Each firm’s data is kept separate from every other firm’s.

    Payment links go to your own Stripe, PayPal or GoCardless account. Evisor never touches the money and takes 0%.

FAQ

The things people actually ask.

Still unsure about something? Ask a person — a real one reads every message.

What do I get on the free plan?

Everything you need to set up and paper a real client: a names-masked preview of businesses near you (in covered areas), the pricing calculator with your floor check, 3 clients, 3 proposals, and 3 engagement letters with e-signature and co-signers. No card, no time limit. Revealing a business’s name and contact details starts on Starter.

Will these businesses actually become clients?

We won’t quote a close rate — we can’t measure one honestly yet, and an invented number is worse than none. What we stand behind: the businesses are real listings in your area, each shows why it’s there and what isn’t known, and when one says “send me something”, you can have a priced, branded proposal in front of them the same day. Finding clients still takes conversations. Evisor makes sure you start the right ones — and don’t lose one to a slow quote.

Where does the business data come from?

Find Clients is built on open business-listing data from the Overture Maps Foundation, with attribution. Contact details are the routes the business has published. Evisor shows them but doesn’t verify them — which is why every business lists what isn’t known, old evidence is flagged, and you should check details before reaching out.

If the data is open, what am I paying for?

The list is only the start. Evisor matches businesses to your area and preferences, shows the case and the gaps for each one, tells you which contact routes are on record before you spend anything, and returns an unlock if a promised route fails. Then it carries the client through pricing, proposal, e-signature, onboarding and renewal. The free plan lets you judge all of that before you pay.

Do other bookkeepers see the same businesses?

They might — Find Clients is built on open listing data, and other Evisor users near you can see the same businesses. What you won’t get is a quote request sold to five firms at once. These businesses haven’t asked anyone for a quote, so there’s no race to answer first; you start the conversation, on your terms.

Will Evisor write my outreach?

Not for today’s leads. You write and send it yourself — from your own email, phone or in person — so the relationship is yours from the first sentence. If you’d like it done with you, Practice Build includes your outreach, written in your voice.

Which areas does Find Clients cover?

Today: Colorado, New York and Oregon. We’re opening area by area. Everything else in Evisor — pricing, proposals, engagement letters, e-signature, onboarding and renewals — works wherever you are. If your area isn’t live yet, start free and use Notify me when this place is live in Find Clients to join the coverage update list.

Does Evisor contact businesses for me?

No, never. You decide who to contact, what to say and how. You’re the sender, so following the rules where you work is up to you — in the US, for example, CAN-SPAM for email.

Isn’t this just another lead list?

A list hands you names. Evisor shows why each business is in front of you, what’s known, what isn’t, and which details are old or conflicting — and it won’t recycle duplicates or quietly broaden your search to fill a quiet area. There are no pay-per-lead credits and no bidding against other bookkeepers for someone’s quote request.

I’m brand new. Is this too much for me?

It’s built for exactly that moment. The pricing calculator works out a fee from your own rates, and you can send a real engagement letter for signature on the free plan. If you get stuck in your first week, we’ll build your first proposal with you on a call.

I already have clients. Can I bring them across?

Yes. Import your client list from a spreadsheet (name, email, business), then paper them properly with engagement letters. On Practice, renewals come around each year so your book is reviewed in one pass.

Are signatures captured in Evisor legally valid?

Evisor captures a simple electronic signature, recognized under laws including the US ESIGN Act, the UK Electronic Communications Act 2000 and the EU eIDAS Regulation. Each one is recorded with the disclosure shown, a fingerprint of the document, the IP address and the time. Some documents legally need a notarized or witnessed signature — don’t rely on any e-signature tool alone for those.

Do my clients need an account or an app?

No. They open a link on any device, read, then accept or sign. On a phone they can draw, upload or photograph a signature.

Can I cancel? Do you offer refunds?

Cancel any time — your plan stays active until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Fees generally aren’t refunded once a billing period starts, which is why the free plan has no time limit: try the parts that matter to you before you pay. Full details are in the refund policy.

How is Practice different from Starter?

Starter is for winning your first clients: 75 reveals a month, your branding, payment links and up to 10 clients. Practice is for running the book: 250 reveals a month, 40 clients, the letter that fills itself from an accepted proposal, scheduled follow-ups, renewals and quick charges.

Do I have to switch from QuickBooks or Xero?

No. Evisor sits alongside your bookkeeping software. It handles finding, pricing, signing and onboarding clients; the books stay where they are.

Why are prices in US dollars?

Evisor bills in US dollars everywhere. If sales tax or VAT applies, it’s calculated and shown at checkout before you pay.
