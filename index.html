02 — Price

Never quote a number you can't defend.

Under-pricing is the quiet killer of a bookkeeping practice, and the fee line is where most people freeze. Evisor turns the job in front of you into three options you can present, checks every one against your own cost floor, and hands you a client-ready quote that drops straight into the proposal.

  • Three options, not one number. Clients who are given a choice buy the middle one. Clients who are given a number negotiate it.
  • A floor you never drop below. Built from your real cost and the actual scope, so a busy month can't turn into an unprofitable client.
  • Market fee benchmarks on Practice. What practices of your size and niche actually charge — the number you need in January when you raise your fees.

This is the most-used tool inside Evisor. It's also the one nobody expects a document product to have — and it's free to use from day one.

If they haven't signed, Evisor follows up on a schedule you set. You never write "just circling back on this" again.

Worth knowing

Two people can both sign the same letter. A married couple filing jointly, two partners, a director and a co-director. Ignition's Solo and Core plans allow one signature per proposal. Co-signers are included on every paid Evisor plan.

On the record

Every signature is captured with the disclosure version shown, the presentation timestamp, the signer's IP, and a document hash recorded before and after signing. Content and signatures are encrypted at rest. Download any signed PDF, any time, forever.
