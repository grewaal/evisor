The process

Exactly what happens from the moment you send your first proposal.

01

Build the proposal

Select from your saved services, add your terms, and hit send. Most bookkeepers send their first proposal in under 10 minutes.

02

Your client signs it

No login, no app download. Your client opens a link, reads the proposal, and signs on any device. Evisor sends automatic reminders if they don’t.

FAQ

Questions you’d ask if we were you.

Is Evisor available right now?

add

Yes. Evisor is live right now. You can sign up, build a real proposal, and send it to a client today — for free. Start with up to 3 clients on the free plan, no card required. Upgrade whenever you’re ready.

Will my clients find this confusing to use?

add

No. Your client gets a link. They open it on any device — phone, tablet, laptop — read the proposal, and tap to sign. No account, no app download, no password needed. Most clients comment on how easy it was compared to what they expected.

Is the e-signature legally binding?

add

Yes. Digital signatures are legally binding under ESIGN (US), eIDAS (EU), and equivalent legislation in most countries. Evisor captures a full audit trail for every signed document — client name, timestamp, IP address — and lets you download a sealed PDF at any time.

How do payments work?

add

Evisor doesn’t take a cut of your payments and doesn’t require you to switch processors. You paste your existing payment link — Stripe, PayPal, Wise, bank transfer, anything — into your proposal. After signing, your client clicks “Pay now” and goes straight to your page. Simple.

Can I use my existing engagement letter?

add

Absolutely. Paste your existing letter into Evisor’s editor — it formats automatically. Save it as your default and you’ll never start from scratch again. Or use our pre-built bookkeeping templates if you’re starting fresh.

Does this replace my practice management tool?

add

Not exactly. Evisor handles the front-end of client engagement: proposals, signatures, onboarding, and initial payment. Once a client is set up, you continue their ongoing work in whatever tool you already use (QBO, Xero, Karbon, etc.). Think of Evisor as what gets new clients in the door and properly set up.

How long does setup take?

add

Most users send their first real proposal within 10 minutes of signing up. Add your services, set your pricing, paste in a template (or use ours), and send. No sales call, no complicated setup, no demo required.

What happens if I outgrow my plan?

add

Upgrade anytime from your account settings — it takes about 30 seconds. Your existing proposals, letters, clients, and templates stay exactly where they are. No data migration, no interruption to your work.

Do you take a percentage of my revenue?

add

No. Never. You pay a flat monthly (or annual) subscription. Evisor takes zero percentage of your client fees. Your payment processor charges their standard rates as usual — but Evisor adds nothing on top of that. Your revenue is yours.
