# V5 — practitioner objections and competitive research

## Method and limits

This is qualitative research for a review candidate, checked during the October 2026 V5 build. It is not an exhaustive market survey, a representative complaint-frequency estimate, a legal review, or a measured conversion experiment. Review excerpts are individual accounts; some are vendor-referred or incentivized. Review sites can syndicate the same reviews, so their counts are not independent samples. Product pages may be cached and must be rechecked before publishing named competitive comparisons.

The page contains no competitor names, logos, ratings or purported exclusivity. Its first-person objections are authored summaries, not quoted testimonials. Private customer correspondence was not copied into this public repository.

## Objection-to-proof mapping

1. Setup burden -> three selectable starting situations; begin with one client or document.
2. Low-trust prospect data -> explicit known/unknown distinction and a research example, not a fabricated demand score.
3. Client friction -> in-page client-view illustration and browser-link description, not an exclusive competitor claim.
4. Repeated proposal/scope work -> recurring/setup/excluded work shown separately; editable templates described only where included.
5. Unclear commitment -> Free displayed prominently; monthly/annual choice with full annual totals.
6. Switching fatigue -> explicit distinction between Evisor and an accounting ledger; no invented QBO/Xero synchronization.
7. Brand/voice concern -> practitioner review and editing, not a promise of custom mail infrastructure.
8. Data and exit anxiety -> relevant policy links; no invented retention guarantees, security badges or cancellation rights.

These design responses are hypotheses. The landing page cannot make an unverified application feature true. The launch owner must validate the target app release and all consequential claims.

## What we deliberately did not add

No named competitor 'gotcha' table, borrowed review rating, fake customer quote, conversion percentage, scarcity timer, guaranteed clients, promise of permanent document storage, or claim to replace an entire accounting/practice-management stack. No copied competitor templates or screenshots. No claim that every complaint found in research is solved by Evisor.

## Validation after founder review

Ask target practitioners to explain what Evisor does, choose a starting point, locate Free, and state the amount/commitment of a paid plan without coaching. Observe errors and hesitations. After launch, measure qualified signup, first meaningful task and retained paid use; do not optimize only CTA clicks. With low traffic, report counts and uncertainty, not an underpowered 'winning' experiment. Preserve V4 as a comparison candidate, not a guaranteed control winner.
