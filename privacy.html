Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. What we collect

We collect your name, email address, and business details when you sign up. We also collect proposal data, signed documents, and audit trail information — including client name, IP address, and timestamp — generated through your use of the Service.

2. How we use your data

We use your data to operate the Service, send you product updates and support messages, and improve Evisor. We do not sell your data to third parties, ever.

3. Payments

Payments are processed through your chosen payment provider (Stripe, PayPal, Wise, or similar). We do not store your payment card details. Your payment provider's own privacy policy applies to data they handle.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use minimal cookies to keep you logged in and understand basic usage patterns. We use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymised data about how visitors use our website — including pages visited, time on site, and general location. This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally. You can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Email and waitlist

If you join our waitlist or subscribe to updates, your email address is stored and managed by Loops.so, our email service provider. Loops processes your email address solely to send you product updates on our behalf. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send. Loops's own privacy policy applies to data they handle.

6. Data retention

Your data is stored on secure, encrypted servers. Proposal documents and audit trails are retained for as long as your account is active, and for up to 90 days after cancellation, after which they are permanently deleted.

7. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing hello@evisor.app. We will respond within 5 business days.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you by email of any significant changes before they take effect.

Questions? Email us at hello@evisor.app.
