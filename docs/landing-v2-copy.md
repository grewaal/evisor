**Recommendation: A.**
- It is the only variant that states the outcome ("find your next clients") and the new mechanism ("evidence") inside the 10-second window, in 8 words.
- It sits at about a grade-3 reading level.
- "First" does two jobs: you see the evidence *before you reach out* and *before you pay*.
- For a burned, sceptical market (Schwartz stage 3), a new mechanism beats a louder promise.

**Why not the others (yet):**
- **B** is the strongest emotional hook for referral-dependent solos, but the outcome is implied rather than stated. It goes first in the A/B queue.
- **C** tells the whole-system story well, but "who do I even contact" needs a beat to parse. Better as the journey-section headline, where it is used.

## 3. Evidence, not a list (Find Clients)

**Eyebrow:** Find Clients
**H2:** Evidence, not a list.
**Lead:** A list gives you names. Evisor gives you the case for each business, gaps included, so you only reach out where it makes sense.

**Annotations on the evidence panel:**
1. **Why it's here.** The match in plain words. If only your area matched, it says so.
2. **Demand.** Marked unknown unless there's published evidence. Evisor never calls a business "ready to buy".
3. **How to reach them.** The contact routes on record (email, phone, website), shown before you spend anything.
4. **What we don't know.** The gaps, listed. Never filled in with guesses.
5. **Check before outreach.** Old or conflicting facts, flagged for you to confirm.
6. **Reveal.** One unlock shows the name and contact details. If a promised route fails its check, the unlock comes back.

**Three things Evisor won't do**
- **Pad a quiet area.** If there aren't many good matches near you, you'll see fewer, not weaker ones.
- **Quietly widen your search.** No recycled duplicates. No silent switch to a bigger radius.
- **Contact anyone for you.** You decide who to reach and how. Evisor never sends a word on your behalf.

**Look before you pay.** On the free plan you can browse the businesses near you with names masked. Reviewing never uses an unlock.

Everything else in Evisor works where you are today: pricing, proposals, engagement letters, e-signature, onboarding and renewals. Start free, then turn on *Notify me* in Find Clients to hear the day it opens."

## 8. Built to be trusted with signatures

**Eyebrow:** Trust
**H2:** Built to be trusted with signatures.
**Lead:** Your engagement letters are legal records. Evisor treats them that way.

- **A record of what was signed.** Each signature is stored with the exact disclosure shown, a SHA-256 fingerprint of what was signed, the signer's IP address and the UTC time. Evisor checks the signer saw the same version before it accepts the signature.
- **Signed means final.** Signed letters and accepted proposals can't be edited afterwards; the database refuses. Changes happen in a new draft.
- **Encrypted where it matters.** Signatures and signed or accepted PDFs are encrypted with AES-GCM. Each firm's data is kept separate from every other firm's.
- **Your clients pay you, not us.** Payment links go to your own Stripe, PayPal or GoCardless account. Evisor never touches the money and takes 0%.
- **Two-factor sign-in.** Turn on an authenticator app. Once it's on, sending and signing ask for it.
- **Your records stay yours.** Export your data from Settings at any time.

**Footnote:** A simple electronic signature, recognised under laws including the US ESIGN Act, the UK Electronic Communications Act 2000 and the EU eIDAS Regulation. Not for documents that legally require a notarised or witnessed signature.

## 11. FAQ

1. **What do I get on the free plan?**
   - Everything you need to set up and paper a real client: a names-masked preview of businesses near you (in covered areas), the pricing calculator with your floor check, 3 clients, and 3 proposals and 3 engagement letters with e-signature and co-signers.
   - No card, no time limit.
   - Revealing a business's name and contact details starts on Starter.
2. **Will these businesses actually become clients?**
   - We won't quote a close rate. We can't measure one honestly yet, and an invented number is worse than none.
   - What we stand behind:
     - the businesses are real listings in your area
     - each one shows why it's there and what isn't known
     - when one says "send me something", you can have a priced, branded proposal in front of them the same day.
   - Finding clients still takes conversations. Evisor makes sure you start the right ones and don't lose one to a slow quote.
3. **Where does the business data come from?**
   - Find Clients is built on open business-listing data from the Overture Maps Foundation, with attribution.
   - Contact details are the routes the business has published. Evisor shows them but doesn't verify them.
   - That's why every business lists what isn't known, and why you should check details before reaching out.
4. **Which areas does Find Clients cover?**
   - Today: Colorado, New York and Oregon. We're opening area by area.
   - Everything else in Evisor works wherever you are.
   - If your area isn't live yet, start free and turn on *Notify me* in Find Clients.
5. **Does Evisor contact businesses for me?**
   - No, never. You decide who to contact, what to say and how.
   - You're the sender, so following the rules where you work is up to you (in the US, for example, CAN-SPAM for email).
6. **Isn't this just another lead list?**
   - A list hands you names. Evisor shows why each business is in front of you, what's known, what isn't, and which details are old or conflicting. It won't pad a quiet area with weaker records.
   - There are no credits and no bidding against other bookkeepers for someone's quote request.
7. **I'm brand new. Is this too much for me?**
   - It’s built for exactly that moment.
     - The pricing calculator works out a fee from your own rates.
     - You can send a real engagement letter for signature on the free plan.
   - If you get stuck in your first week, we’ll build your first proposal with you on a call.
8. **I already have clients. Can I bring them across?**
   - Yes. Import your client list from a spreadsheet (name, email, business).
   - Then paper them properly with engagement letters. On Practice, renewals come round each year.
9. **Are signatures captured in Evisor legally valid?**
   - Evisor captures a simple electronic signature, recognised under laws including the US ESIGN Act, the UK Electronic Communications Act 2000 and the EU eIDAS Regulation.
   - Each one is recorded with the disclosure shown, a fingerprint of the document, the IP address and the time.
   - Some documents legally need a notarised or witnessed signature. Don't rely on any e-signature tool alone for those.
10. **Do my clients need an account or an app?**
    - No. They open a link on any device, read, then accept or sign.
    - On a phone they can draw, upload or photograph a signature.
11. **Can I cancel? Do you offer refunds?**
    - Cancel any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.
    - Fees generally aren't refunded once a billing period starts, which is why the free plan has no time limit.
    - Full details are in the refund policy.
12. **How is Practice different from Starter?**
    - Starter is for winning your first clients: 75 reveals a month, your branding, payment links and up to 10 clients.
    - Practice is for running the book: up to 250 reveals, 40 clients, the letter that fills itself from an accepted proposal, scheduled follow-ups, renewals and quick charges.
13. **Why are prices in US dollars?**
    - Evisor bills in US dollars everywhere.
    - If sales tax or VAT applies, it's calculated and shown at checkout before you pay.
